Who we are

RM Travel is a small team of four who got tired of holiday sites that make every trip look expensive. We built this guide to do the opposite. It's meant to help you find a good holiday without spending more than you need to.

What we do

We pick out destinations and tell you what you actually get for your money. Here's what you can expect from us:

  • Prices shown up front, with no surprise extras at the end
  • Honest write-ups, so if a place isn't worth it, we'll say so
  • Trips that suit solo travellers, couples, families and big groups
  • Budget stays that still feel like a treat

What we believe

A good holiday doesn't have to cost a fortune. Most of the time, what makes a trip memorable is the company, the food and a bit of time away. So we look for places that feel like a treat, even when the price doesn't.

Who's behind it

RM Travel was put together by Sean, Cathal, Elliot and James. We each built a different part of the site, and we're still learning as we go. If something looks off or you've got an idea, we'd like to hear it.
